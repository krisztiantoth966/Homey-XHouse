XHouse brings compatible gate and garage controllers into your Homey setup, so opening and closing the gate can become part of everyday routines and automations. Use the gate from the Homey app, keep track of its current state and combine it with Flow for arrivals, departures and security routines.

The app connects directly to the XHouse / Giigle IoT cloud and does not require Home Assistant. It is a community-built integration and requires a working XHouse account and internet connection.
